# Introduction to Data Science

This document provides a foundational overview of Data Science, detailing its core mathematical and computational components, various machine learning algorithms, and the complete end-to-end lifecycle of a data science project, alongside a practical business implementation use-case.

## Table of Contents

*   **1. Core Components of Data Science:** An introduction to the foundational pillars, including Probability & Statistics, Linear Algebra, Computer Science, and Machine Learning.
*   **2. Machine Learning Types & Algorithms:** A breakdown of Supervised, Unsupervised, Semi-Supervised, and Reinforcement Learning methodologies and their common algorithms.
*   **3. Data Science Architecture:** An overview of how data moves from raw historical sources through feature engineering and modeling to final action and deployment.
*   **4. The Project Life Cycle:** The six critical steps of a project: Define the Goal, Collect and Manage Data, Build a Model, Evaluate Model, Present Results, and Deploy Model.
*   **5. Business Implementation Use-Case:** A practical example applying the data science life cycle to a banking scenario aimed at classifying and reducing bad loans.
